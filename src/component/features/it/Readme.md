
## Model Business Service: 
1) First pane tree for Model Business Service is rendered from FeatureContainer>ExplorerTreeContainer 
2) Second pane tree of DC explorere will be rendered from ExplorerContainer>DCExplorerContainer 
3) Third pane will be rendered from LayoutContainer>BSDiagram based on selected node from Business Service Tree selected 
4) AddNewBusinessService form is handled commonly and will be shown in sidebar
